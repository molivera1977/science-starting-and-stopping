      End of Day 1

      Nice work. You finished both of today’s tests.

      Next time you will do two more tests
      1. The ramp test. You put a car at the top of a ramp and let it go. You do not push it at all — gravity pulls it down on its own. You try three ramp heights: 1 book high, then 2 books, then 3 books. Twice each, so 6 runs. You are finding out whether a taller ramp sends the car farther.
      2. The truck test. You push a car, then you push a truck, with the exact same push on the exact same wood. The truck is the heavier one. Twice each, so 4 pushes. You are finding out whether the heavy one goes as far as the light one.
      3. Then you explain what you found. You will not have to write a paragraph. You pick your answer three times: what you think is true, then the numbers from your own table that show it, then why it happened. The site puts your three picks together into one explanation and shows it to you.

      That is 10 runs altogether — fewer than today.
