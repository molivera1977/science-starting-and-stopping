The big question for these three days

An object is a thing. A ball is an object. A car is an object. A book is an object.

You will be the scientist. A scientist is a person who asks a question, then tests to find the answer.

You will run tests. A test is a way to try something and see what happens. In each test you will measure how far a thing travels before the thing stops. How far a thing travels is called the distance. The distances you measure will tell you the answer.

Why should you care?

Stopping is everywhere in your life. Sometimes stopping is what keeps you safe.

Starting and Stopping

Today you are a scientist who studies how things move. You will push a cart and roll a car down a ramp. Every time, you will measure how far the cart or the car travels. Then you will use the distances you measured to figure out what makes a moving object stop.

Driving question

What causes some objects to stop and other objects to keep moving?
